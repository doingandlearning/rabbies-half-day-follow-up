# Module 4 — Open Q&A & Close: What to Trial Next

**Closing session — AI-Assisted Development for Laravel Teams: Half-Day Follow-Up**

## Overview

The close of the session. An open, unscripted Q&A driven entirely by what the room brings — including anything parked during Module 1's retro — followed by agreeing one or two concrete things the team will trial before the next check-in, with named ownership. This is the most actionable close of the whole engagement: it turns everything covered today into commitments someone is actually accountable for.

## Learning objectives

By the end of this session, participants will be able to:

- Get a direct answer to any real friction or question raised since the core day that hasn't yet had a home
- Name one or two specific things to trial before the next check-in, including a first concrete step on evals
- State clearly who owns the spec template going forward, who owns evals, and who's watching for sign-off being skipped

## Suggested running time

30 minutes

## Session structure

| Section | Time |
|---|---|
| Open Q&A | 15 min |
| Close: agree what to trial next | 15 min |
